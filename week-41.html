Week 415 October 2026Prepared by: Jakarta Marketing

1Executive Summary

China lane competition

More competition on the China–Jakarta lane. HMM, PIL and RCL open a new Qingdao–Shanghai–Nansha–Jakarta loop (first sailing 8 November). Meratus starts a Shanghai–Nansha–HCM–Jakarta service.

National exports up

Indonesia exports in August were USD 26.61 billion, up 6.72% YoY. Exports to China in January–August were up 17.42% YoY.

Jakarta exports down

DKI Jakarta-origin exports in August were USD 1.18 billion, down 19.88% YoY. DKI Jakarta exports to Thailand were up 57% YTD.
