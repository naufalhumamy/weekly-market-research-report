Sinokor Jakarta — Weekly Market Report

Week 4213 October 2026Prepared by: Jakarta Marketing

1Executive Summary

Palm oil to China under pressure

Indonesian palm product exports in August were 3.59Mt (up 12.56% MoM), but Malaysia has record stocks of 3.51Mt and Indonesian export duty and levy are higher in October. Chinese buyers may split orders toward Malaysian product.

Rubber at a 13-year high

Natural rubber was 264.80 US cents/kg on 12 October (up 53.6% YoY), driven by Thai flooding and rain. Cargo value is up, but volume is uncertain because Chinese tyre demand is soft.

Southeast Asia port delays

Tanjung Priok is running normally. Delays continue in Southeast Asia: congestion at Ho Chi Minh/Cai Mep and flood disruption on the landside at Laem Chabang.

Palm oil and shortening · Shanghai/Shekou

  • Indonesian palm product exports (August): 3.59Mt, up 12.56% MoM. Refined products 2.64Mt, up 15.12%.
  • Export value January–August: USD 27.55 billion, up 11.14% YoY.
  • Malaysia September stocks: record 3.51Mt, up 24.54% MoM.
  • China was not among the destinations with the largest increases in August. China-lane volumes depend on existing contracts and Q4 food restocking before Chinese New Year.

Natural rubber · Japan, Busan/Incheon, Shanghai/Shekou

  • Price on 12 October: 264.80 US cents/kg, up 12.2% MoM and up 53.6% YoY.
  • Drivers: flooding in Thailand, rain and El Niño risk.
  • Chinese tyre demand remains soft. Buyers may delay purchases at current prices.

Cocoa · Shanghai/Shekou, some to Japan and Korea

  • October cocoa bean reference price: USD 6,057.87/MT, up 7.49%.
  • ICE cocoa on 12 October: USD 5,665.89/t, on West African supply risk.
  • Jakarta volume is mainly processed cocoa (butter, powder, liquor). Processed-cocoa container volumes are expected to be stable.

Other

  • Rupiah closed at Rp17,913/USD on 12 October, slightly weaker. This supports exporters' price competitiveness.
  • Trade Expo Indonesia (TEI): 14–18 October at ICE BSD, Tangerang.

5Competitor & Service News

  • No new service, rotation, blank sailing or surcharge news touching Jakarta this week for KMTC, HMM, CK Line, Namsung, SITC, COSCO, Jin Jiang, ONE, RCL, Interasia or Gold Star Line.
  • Coming up: HMM/PIL/RCL Qingdao–Shanghai–Nansha–Jakarta loop starts 8 November.
  • COSCO launched a new Shekou–Nansha–Port Klang service (7 October). It does not call Jakarta.
  • ONE and HMM are expected to be the next carriers to return to Suez routing. No direct effect on our lanes.
  • Intra-Asia: congestion at Singapore, Port Klang and Ho Chi Minh/Cai Mep. Feeder capacity is tight in the Bangkok-max segment. Chinese port backlogs eased during National Day.

6Regulation & Port Update

  • Tanjung Priok: operations normal. No congestion, strike or INSW/CEISA outage reported.
  • Weather: BMKG warned of waves up to 4m in some Indonesian waters on 10–13 October. The Java Sea had a high-wind warning.
  • ODOL (overloaded trucks): Kemenhub plans enforcement at ports ("Zero ODOL 2027"). The first trial is at Tanjung Perak, not Priok. No dates given.
  • Laem Chabang: port is operating. The Lat Krabang–Laem Chabang rail shuttle is suspended and road moves are slower due to flooding.
  • Palm oil (October): CPO reference price USD 1,042.15/MT, export duty USD 178/MT, levy 12.5%. November reference price is due around the end of October.
  • Cocoa beans (October): export duty 7.5% and levy 7.5%.
  • Rubber: no new export rule.
